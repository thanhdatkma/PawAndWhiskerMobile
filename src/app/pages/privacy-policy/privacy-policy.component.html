Your Privacy Matters

At Paws & Whiskers, we treat your pet's data with the same love and care we give to our furry friends.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Paws & Whiskers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information. Our commitment is to ensure your pet care journey is safe, transparent, and built on trust.

2. Data Collection

We collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

Account Details

Name, email, phone number, and account credentials used to identify you.

Pet Information

Breed, age, health records, and activity tracking data for your furry friends.

3. Data Usage

Your data helps us personalize your experience. We use it to suggest products, remind you of vet appointments, track pet activity, and improve our overall app performance to serve you better.

4. Data Protection

"We protect your data with the same intensity we use to protect our own pets."

We employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Your information is stored on secure, monitored servers.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with trusted partners necessary to provide our core services.

6. User Rights

You have full control. You can request to view, update, or delete your personal data at any time through account settings.

Acceptance of Privacy Policy

By continuing to use PetPulse, you acknowledge that you have read and understood how we handle your data.
